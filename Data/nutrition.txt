Nutrition science explores how diet impacts human health performance and longevity. A balanced diet rich in fruits vegetables whole grains lean proteins and healthy fats supports immune function and reduces disease risk. Ultra-processed foods high in sugar salt and saturated fat are linked to obesity diabetes and cardiovascular disease. Intermittent fasting and ketogenic diets have gained popularity with mixed scientific support depending on individual health goals. Gut microbiome research reveals how the trillions of bacteria in our digestive system influence mood immunity and metabolism. Food deserts in low-income communities limit access to fresh nutritious food creating health disparities. Sustainable eating patterns that reduce meat consumption also help lower agriculture's environmental footprint.
