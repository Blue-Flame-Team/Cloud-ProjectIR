Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns.
Human activities such as burning fossil fuels are the main drivers of climate change.
The greenhouse effect traps heat in the Earth atmosphere causing global warming.
Rising sea levels threaten coastal cities and low-lying island nations worldwide.
Extreme weather events like hurricanes, droughts, and floods are becoming more frequent.
Melting polar ice caps and glaciers contribute to rising ocean levels.
Carbon dioxide and methane are the primary greenhouse gases causing global warming.
Renewable energy sources like solar and wind power can help reduce carbon emissions.
The Paris Agreement aims to limit global warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels.
Deforestation reduces the number of trees that absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.
