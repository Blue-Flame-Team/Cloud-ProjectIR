Space exploration involves the use of astronomy and space technology to explore outer space.
NASA is the United States government agency responsible for the civilian space program.
The Moon is the only celestial body beyond Earth that humans have visited.
Mars is the most explored planet in our solar system with multiple rovers studying its surface.
The International Space Station is a modular space station in low Earth orbit.
Black holes are regions of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing can escape.
The Milky Way galaxy contains between 100 and 400 billion stars including our Sun.
SpaceX is a private aerospace company that has revolutionized space travel with reusable rockets.
The James Webb Space Telescope can observe the universe in infrared light revealing distant galaxies.
Astronauts experience weightlessness in space because they are in a constant state of free fall.
