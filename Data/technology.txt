Technology refers to the application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes.
The internet has transformed communication commerce and access to information worldwide.
Smartphones have become essential devices combining communication computing and entertainment.
Cloud computing allows users to store and access data and programs over the internet.
Cybersecurity protects computer systems networks and data from digital attacks and damage.
The Internet of Things connects everyday devices to the internet enabling smart homes and cities.
Blockchain technology provides a secure decentralized way to record transactions and data.
5G networks promise faster speeds lower latency and greater capacity for wireless communications.
Virtual reality and augmented reality are changing entertainment education and training.
Quantum computing uses quantum mechanics to solve complex problems much faster than traditional computers.
