Economics is the study of how people allocate scarce resources to satisfy their unlimited wants.
The global economy consists of all the economic activities carried out by countries worldwide.
Inflation refers to the general increase in prices and fall in the purchasing value of money.
Stock markets allow companies to raise capital by selling shares to the public.
Unemployment measures the number of people actively seeking work but unable to find jobs.
International trade allows countries to specialize in producing goods they are most efficient at making.
Central banks control monetary policy by setting interest rates and managing money supply.
Gross domestic product measures the total value of goods and services produced in a country.
Cryptocurrency is a digital form of currency that uses cryptography for secure transactions.
Supply and demand are fundamental forces that determine prices in a market economy.
