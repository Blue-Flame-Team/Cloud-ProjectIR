Electric vehicles are reshaping the automotive industry as battery technology improves and prices fall. Tesla pioneered the mainstream EV market while legacy automakers like Ford, Volkswagen, and General Motors are committing hundreds of billions to electrification. Governments worldwide are setting deadlines to phase out internal combustion engines and building public charging infrastructure. Range anxiety once a major barrier is diminishing as modern EVs can travel over 400 miles on a single charge. The transition to electric mobility reduces greenhouse gas emissions from transportation one of the largest sources of carbon pollution. Lithium-ion batteries present supply chain challenges as demand for cobalt nickel and lithium surges. Vehicle-to-grid technology could allow EV batteries to stabilize electrical grids during peak demand.
