Football is the most popular sport in the world with billions of fans globally.
The FIFA World Cup is held every four years and is the most watched sporting event on earth.
A standard football match consists of two teams with eleven players each.
The game is played on a rectangular grass field with a goal at each end.
Players score by getting the ball into the opposing team's goal.
The offside rule is one of the most debated rules in football.
Famous clubs include Real Madrid, Barcelona, Manchester United, and Bayern Munich.
Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo are considered two of the greatest footballers of all time.
The Premier League in England is one of the most competitive football leagues in the world.
Football requires teamwork, stamina, speed, and technical skill to play at a high level.
